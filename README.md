# 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis

**Data Analysis & Business Intelligence Project**

Comprehensive analysis of pizza sales data to uncover key business insights, trends, and performance metrics.

---

## 📋 Project Objective

The goal of this project is to analyze pizza sales data to help the restaurant understand:
- Revenue trends and total performance
- Customer ordering patterns (by day and hour)
- Best-selling pizzas and categories
- Product size preferences
- Opportunities for business growth

---

## 📊 Key Insights

- **Total Revenue**: **$69,793.30**
- **Total Orders**: **18,445**
- **Total Pizzas Sold**: **4,232**
- **Average Order Value**: **$37.83**
- **Average Pizzas per Order**: **2.29**

### Category Performance
- **Classic** — Highest revenue
- **Chicken**, **Supreme**, and **Veggie** follow closely

### Size Performance
- **Large** pizzas generate the highest revenue
- **Medium** and **Regular** sizes also perform well

### Top Selling Pizzas
- The Thai Chicken Pizza
- The Barbecue Chicken Pizza
- The California Chicken Pizza
- The Pepperoni Pizza
